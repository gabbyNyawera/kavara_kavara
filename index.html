Where We Began

A culture that found its fire

KAVARA KAVARA began in the heart of Ghana, where the rhythms of the day were set by heat and light — the kettle on the coals, the table under the mango tree, the long afternoons when nobody needed to be anywhere.

That memory travelled with us, and we rebuilt it around our own fire. The name echoes the sound of rain on a tin roof — patient, warm, impossible to hurry.
